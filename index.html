    FIELD MANUAL // ASCENSION TOWER

    Everything in the tower, and what it does

    Break it shoot or throw at it Avoid it change lanes or put it out Collect it run through or shoot it Your tools what you have

    Glass sphere // 1

    Standard throw. Punches through panes and keeps going; ricochets off walls and ceilings.

    Cryo sphere // 2

    Puts out every fire within 4.8 m, freezes falling debris and machinery, knocks people down.

    Shock sphere // 3

    Shatters all glass within 5 m. One-hit reinforced glass.

    Sphere cache

    The floating cyan crystal. Shoot it or run into it for spheres.

    Prism split

    Gold capsule. Every throw splits into three spheres. 10 s.

    Thermal sight

    Orange capsule. See through smoke and the dark. 12 s.

    Kinetic shield

    Green capsule. Absorbs the next two impacts. 25 s.

    Overdrive

    Pink capsule. Free throws, faster running, heavier hits. 8 s.

    Records

    Gold holograms in light beams, in every sector: plant logs, patient records, case files, flight records. Run through one to read it.

    Switch cells

    Glass cells on stems. Break one and the gate it holds opens - three stand between you and the lift.

    Pressure cells

    Small glass cells everywhere: score, and three spheres back each.

    Pistons and presses

    Slam down on a rhythm. Read the timing or change lanes; cryo freezes them for a few seconds.

    Barriers

    Low ones you jump (Space or W), high ones you slide under (Shift or S).

    Plant charges

    Near the end Vale's charges arm and the shutters start sealing: a clock to the lift. Break the extraction valve.

    The fire front

    It's behind you the whole way. Slow down and it catches you.

    Checkpoints

    Each new section of the Labs (the corridor, the blackout, the stairwell) is a checkpoint. Caught after one, you start again there, not at the top.

    Fallen ducts

    Jump onto them and off the far side - running into one is a hit. At the fire crossings, the duct is the only bridge.

    Patients

    Lurch out into your lane. In the dark they stalk you, following your lane until the last moment: shoot them (two hits) or sidestep late.

    The launcher's light

    When the power dies, your beam is the only light. Spheres glow - firing ahead is scouting.

    Glass and lasers

    Panes break (glass spheres go straight through); laser grids you jump or slide.

    Glass pane

    Cyan rim means breakable. Shoot it for score, or crash through it and lose 15 integrity.

    Reinforced glass and doors

    Thicker and greener. The first hit cracks it, the second breaks it. A shock sphere breaks it in one.

    Sphere cache

    Floating crystal. Shoot it for +3 spheres. Your only real source of ammunition.

    Solid hazards

    Dark steel with amber stripes: carts, cabinets, racks, fallen beams. They never break. Change lanes.

    Fire

    Burns integrity while you stand in it and thickens the smoke. Put it out with a sprinkler or a cryo sphere.

    Sprinkler bulb

    The small red glass bulb on the ceiling. Shoot it to flood the area and put out the fires below it.

    Air vent

    A round frosted cover in the wall with a glowing cyan ring and a SMOKE VENT sign, angled toward you. The ring pulses when the air is smoky. Throw a sphere at it to pull the smoke out; the ring turns green.

    Ceiling collapse

    A red ring on the floor means something is about to fall there. Beams block the lane. Falling glass can be shot mid-air.

    Rotating sculpture

    A steel pole blocks the centre lane; glass blades sweep the outer lanes. Shoot the blades or time your lane.

    Serum capsules

    Prism split (triple throw), thermal sight (see through smoke), kinetic shield (absorbs two hits), overdrive (free throws, faster).

    Case files

    Five gold holograms, each standing in a column of light: two in the ward, one on the skybridge, two in the atrium. Run through one or shoot it to read what happened here.

    Specimen tanks

    Glass tanks along the walls. Optional score.

    Lift gate

    Break locks I, II, III in order. Wrong order is rejected. You have seven seconds once you reach the gate.

    Sprint, brake, focus

    W sprints, S brakes (the bridge collapses behind you, so do not brake long). Hold right mouse to slow time.

    Patients

    Climb out of the hatches, stalk, wind up and charge in a straight line. Sidestep one near an open ledge and it goes over.

    Open hatches

    The square holes the patients climb out of go straight down into the building. Step into one and you fall.

    Scientists

    Fire slow orbs from their gadgets - dodge them (Space). Three hits each.

    Gas canisters

    Red, by the cover. Shoot one and everyone near it goes down - chains into the next. Not too close yourself.

    Supply drop

    A flare, a crate under a parachute: spheres and a serum. Walk into them or shoot them.

    The brute

    The last wave's big one: six hits, charges harder. Use a canister or the ledge.

    The helicopter

    Clear the roof and it lands; still fighting when it comes and it hangs off the ledge - jump for the ladder.
